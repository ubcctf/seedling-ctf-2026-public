



## Forensics Challenge : drawing_tablet

Generally this is pcap file should be enough for most solves.


**Catetory:** Forensics 

**Difficulty:** Medium 

### Description 



Someone borrowed the lab's Wacom drawing tablet, plugged it in over **USB**, and
scribbled a secret.  The drawing was never saved, but a capture of the tablet's
USB traffic survived.

Help you prof to recover what was written.

Flag is in the format of `maple{...}`.

You have a handout of the traffic in a pcap file.
